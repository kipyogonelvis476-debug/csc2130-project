About Farm Records

Technical Terms

HTML
HyperText Markup Language, used to create the structure of web pages.
CSS
Cascading Style Sheets, used to style and design web pages.
Database
A system used to store and organize farm records and information.
Farm Records
Information about crops, livestock, expenses, sales, and other farm activities.

Our Mission

Our mission is to provide farmers with easy management of their farming activities throughout the year to avoid maximum wastige and unnecessary losses.

Our Vision

Our vision is to make farm management simple and efficient for all farmers.And to ensure every farmer succeed in their farming activities.

Farming is very important for every farmer. We encourage continuous production and our nation excellence for a better future and growing economy.

How to Use Our Farm Records System

  1. Open the Farm Records application
  2. Log in to your account
  3. Enter farm details
  4. Record livestock and crop information
  5. View and update farm records
